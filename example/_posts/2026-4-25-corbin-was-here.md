Jimmy went hunting early in the morning and caught one of the squirrels that Iris carries around with her. Its meat makes for a delicious bacon.

We chatted with him a while and learned his side of things. Evidentally he'd joined up with the Mugger's Guild thinking they were a collectibles group (he has a prized ceramic mug), and was dissapointed to find out otherwise. He and Jasper seem to have left the Rust Knights since the last time we spoke. He seems to be looking for a bit of peace around here, though his brother seems to be looking for trouble.

And on that note, it seems that the siblings have an interesting relationship. Jimmy arrived in this realm first, and was the younger sibling, but Jasper was also recruited and transported into this realm, and now the two are drastically different ages. It seems either time within this realm moves slower in comparison to other realms, or their home realm moves faster.

I was warned about such a descrepincy before I made my first leap between realms, though truthfully I've never gone back to any realm I've visited to verify that. I don't really want to dwell on it much if I'm being honest. The thought that my siblings back home could be old or dead by the time I return... it's not the best thing to think about. I figured they now think that I've died in the war, and I never really thought to return home or give them any crumbs to explain where I've gone and who I've become. Honestly, writing it out just now it seems cruel, but I hope they never find this journal or learn my pseudonym. I feel for Jimmy and Jasper though. Jimmy seems to want to go home.

We briefly thought to send them up north to Riawat, but... last we saw these two they were hunting lightless. As much as I wanted to trust them, I didn't want to damn a safehaven on the odds that the unconscious brother could mind his manners. We ended up taking a poll on potentially giving these two access to the "Tear in Reality", and sending them home. Dedrik and Grem voted against the measure though, and Luisa and Noggin abstained from the vote. I gave the Tear to Dedrik afterwards, since he seemed to most intent on making his way home. I can't say I hold them in high regards after the vote, but at the end of the day this group is temporary. We'll part ways in our own time, and I should be grateful for the help and guidance these people have given me so far.

I must admit though, being around Jimmy has reminded me of what it's like to talk with normal... humans. It's been a while since I've encountered just... another ordinary man out on the trail. I think I've come a ways in not being overwhelmed by the fantastical people and places I've seen, but there's something comforting in a bit of the mundane.

We ended up talking with Jimmy for a while about our plans ahead, and decided the best route forward would be heading to the Gutter by the plantation. He said it was not an unwelcoming place, which is about as good as we've gotten so far on our travels. We planned to stop by and drop him and Jasper off so they could rest for awhile at the local clinic.

On our way over though, we encountered a rather strange man. A courier stopped us along our way, and delivered a letter to Luisa. We weren't in a great rush, so we ended up talking for a while with the man.

He introduced himself as Connor, and he appears to be a human man like myself. Evidentally Connor had been a monk living an isolated lifestyle not by choice, originating from some fantastical world with floating islands and very few people. He has a light similar to mine, and evidentally has been on this world for quite some time travelling with the local caravans.

Luisa, of course, challenged him to a duel. To our surprise, the monk not only accepted the challenge, but BEAT Luisa without ever drawing a weapon. I'm more than impressed. Evidentally he was trained by his parents, and cannot divulge the secrets of his arts, but... well even just seeing a human take down a dragon like Luisa, it's a fascinating discovery.

Connor tagged along with our crew after the debacle, partially out of convenience and I suspect largely because he seems a lonely fellow who likes to talk. I wonder what else he may divulge.

We headed down together into the Gutter and found it to be filled with toxic gas. The gas appeared to have a hallucinogenic property, causing visions of faces and whispers upon inhalation. The residents of the town were happy to point us to where we could acquire better clothing and masks for dealing with the fumes, and we took them up on their offer.

The lady originally in charge of this town, Lizzy, has apparently been disposed. Jimmy wasn't keen on the old leader, and no one we talked to in town spoke a good word about her, though we failed to learn why. The new leader, a scientist (?) Tanggal named Mixie was recieved much better, and was evidentally leading her people in the "Great Work".

We fit in nicely with the locals, and found a place for Jasper to recover. And, for the first time in a fair while, we found ourselves with a bit of extra time on our hands.

While we were burning time, Luisa suggested we might visit and lend a hand to Mixie. The scientist was running low on supplies, and sent us to grab some explosive charges from her vault.

During our visit to the vault, we noticed that several items had already been stolen from the building. While the door was sealed when we arrived, we noticed small, claw-like scratches along the buttons and shelves, like a creature had been through and carelessly left tracks behind. A few items were missing as well, including a bottle of Panacea [part of this section has been erased], a "False Cure", several artifacts, and a "False God" in a wing dedicated to dangerous specimens. 

We did suspect on our way back that the culprit might have been Corbin, who had plans for robbing this very vault. We had not anticipated him to strike so soon after his defeat, but perhaps he recovered faster than we anticipated. That man truly is a dastard if so, and got away with quite a bit of interesting items that may have proven helpful in our own journeys.

We were puzzled over this robbery, and reported it to Mixie afterwards. She seemed distressed immediately, and became increasingly panicked over the missing Panacea specifically. We took the explosive charges to her lab while she ran back to investigate and hunt down the missing vial.

Mixie's panic over the Panacea did make me panic a bit as well. We still know very little about the medicine gifted to us by those elves, and... giving Mixie's reaction to how dangerous the substance is, I'm inclined on perhaps keeping this substance under wraps until we know exactly what it is.

Some of the townsfolk we asked about the Panacea filled in a bit of information for us. Apparently the Panacea that Mixie has is somewhat different from ours, with a red gem/light running through it. Supposedly, if you were to hold two pieces of Panacea together, they seem to react and try to rebind with one another. It's a very curious substance.

I'd originally wondered if maybe the panacea was a part of the earth in some way, seeing that this world appears to be a living substance. Perhaps its god-flesh instead? Maybe a part of an immortal oni or god that wishes to rejoin with itself? The colored variation of the substance also interests me. If you die and consume the red panacea, do you gain a light from Tazaam? 

It's still all speculation at this point, and I think if I wish to learn more, I should do so subtely as to not cause unneeded concern to others.

In any case, we spent the rest of the day retrieving Jimmy and Jasper, and heading out of town. Our group headed out towards a yellow outpost with docks and boats aplenty, though we got waylaid in a rather nasty swamp on our way there.

The fumes at the swamp were much, much stronger than in the city, and the pollution of the swamp was so thick that Bingus could practically walk across the water. We made it a decent ways into the crossing but Grem succumbed to the toxic gasses, and a large corpse-like fish nearly gobbled her up. Thankfully, a good fireball from Noggin remedied the situation (and set half the swamp on fire).

We rushed to the other side of the swamp and tore our way to freedom. Just past the swamp we landed in clean waters, where we were picked up by a boat passing by.
