From the (temporary) desk of Gremlina Artemisia

After weighing the prospect in my mind, I have decided to begin writing my memoirs. I am unsure when I will be able to abscond from this accursed locale. Frankly, there have been times when I feared that I may in fact already be deceased and these unfortunate events are merely a punishment from the gods for my actions whilst in the mortal realm. 

My deep and abiding love for the praeternatural has been a part of me since infancy. This fanaticism was nurtured by the expansive libraries in Windvale and the eager ears of my peers and dear little brother Grim. His earnestness was perhaps the most affirming of all. And the fact that he was quite gullible. This trait however, was unfortunately dulled over time. 

One notable annecdote close encounter with the otherworldly came when I was but a young pup of 113 years. the restlessness this ill portends caused among the human farmers was creating a stir among the inteligencia in Windvale. My father, Terrell Artemsia, Professor of Transmutation Magics and Arcana at the Benevolent Academy of Advanced Study of Magics and the Supernatural Realms, was one of those looking into solutions for the bovine culling. 

One evening, I took it upon myself to provide field research into my personal hypothesis. The poor cows were the prey of the Vampyr, those accursed, awful children of the night whose unquentiable hunger is only sated by the life's blood of mortals. Sweet, little Grim joined my expedition after working up the courage. I will admit his assistance was sorely needed. While I was quite sprightly in those year's, I was unfortunately not strong enough to gain entrance into the abandoned farm I planned to investigate, Grim was hardier and stronger in arm than I. 

The farm was recently abandoned but the fearful frenzy of abandonment left it in disrepair. Weeds and overgrown crops rotted in the yard and sundry items were scattered about having been left behind in the tumult. It was eerily quiet, no creature of this plane dared make a sound as Grim and I walked up the path. The farmhouse door was thankfully not difficult to enourage to open so I searched inside while Grim searched the barn. The inside of the farmhouse was a mess, dishes with week's old scraps of food collected bugs and the bedrooms looked as though a squall had whipped up the contents of every dresser. It was all so dull, no blood nor sign of supernatural struggle. After searching for what felt like most of the night, I was interupted by Grim, eyes wide and hands like cold, clammy fish. Though his babbling was spotted our quarry! Despite his protest, I ran to the barn. The vampire had already fled the premesis having been spotted, much to my chagrin.

After that, the mysterious deaths of livestock ceased, though Grim was quite unsettled after the encounter. He had a haunted look in his eye and was prone to being startled by sudden sounds or quick movement, only comfortable in bright sunlight. Each day after school, he would go out to the garden and sit until sunset. At times I thought I could hear him speaking, though to whom I was not sure. All I could hear besides his soft voice was the twittering of birds. These events spurred me to continue my education (the erased and crossed through several times)

My mother, Grizelda was a shining star of the Windvale Multi-Chime orchestra and had passed on her musical talents to me. This has been of tremendous value in my many travels these past 250 years. One other notable encounter was with the wretched Squonk, a creature known for it's pityable it by through the acquaintances made at local taverns, lips loosened by drink and merry tune.

One evening, while tracking the creature in the forest surrounding some backwards hamlet, I heard a horrible wail moon, I saw the squonk slain upon the ground in a small clearing. As a I hurried to examine the creature, I heard an odd voice shout from the brush nearby. 

"Don't touch it Gremlina, its covered in poisonous mucous!

"Perplexed, I stayed my hand and called for the mysterious poacher to reveal themselves, for they had slain a rare and mystical beast this night. I was shocked to see Grim, older and much taller, armed with a crossbow and festooned with various survival gear. 

"Grimbald, do you understand what you've done? There may be only one of these creatures left. If you have decided to live like a vagabond-"

"I was not hunting this animal for survival, Gremlina. I have a duty to protect this ecosystem and this creature was a threat. It was releasing toxins into the water."

"But what right to you have to decide what is a threat to this ecosystem as you call it?" 

"I have a," Grim paused and fidgeted with a roughly carved pendant shaped like a small bird hanging from his neck, "charter or a sort to keep this region balanced. You can examine the beast for scientific purposes if you'd like."

Grim looked at me, sad and owlish. So, to make peace, I agreed to settle with an anotomical study of the poor creature. Both of us separated way after that, I decided a new region would be best. Fresh faces and fresh pockets. 

And now I am here.
